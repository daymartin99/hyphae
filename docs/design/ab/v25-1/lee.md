# HYPHAE playtest: Lee (skeptic), round v2.5-1

## 1. Stop point
I stopped at **about 610 played minutes** (roughly 10 h on the game clock, breaks included). I quit; I didn't hit the cap or the tool limit. The last straw was the contract screen: the layout jumped under my thumb for the third time in that screen, so my SIGN tap opened the aspen card and nothing got signed. Before that I'd had about 20 taps of the game refusing contracts in poetry when I was under the limit it showed me. By then the only way forward was minerals, which only come from contracts, and I was fighting the UI to get them.

## 3. What was fun
1. **The writing reacting to what I did.** "you paid for something that was free an hour ago" (after I bought litter back), "the third winter. you knew it was coming and you bought in october", "the price went up while you were buying. you did that." *Surprise and being noticed.* These made a decision feel like it had a consequence and a witness. It's the only thing that made me smile in an idle game in years.
2. **Standing Order + Dormancy Clause (~min 285).** "It buys badly and it never sleeps, and you will take that trade. (Automated, 8% worse than you)" and "Sleep does not break a promise. (No offline defaults)". *Relief and a sense of growth.* My two biggest gripes, restocking every 10 minutes and being punished for closing the app, each had a purchasable fix, and the fix is honest about what it costs. Paying 8% to stop babysitting is a real trade-off.
3. **Patches as milestones.** "a second place. the same rules, six metres away", "where the wind did your work for you". *Anticipation.* The "next: patch … 75 ∴ short, about 9:15 at this rate" line gave me a concrete target with a countdown, which is the right kind of goal for someone checking in between other things.

## 4. What wasn't fun
1. **The contract screen.** Sliders move when I try to scroll. "Coverage" says "you make 0.00 sug/s … this defaults" while the sugar sheet says +43 sug/s net. SIGN refuses with "the soil does not give. not yet. (11.4 sug/s at most, 5 seasons)" when I'm under 11.4, and aspen's SIGN was greyed for no reason I could read. *Confusion and friction.* Pretty words are not a substitute for telling me the rule. This is where I quit.
2. **The layout jumps under taps.** The header grows and shrinks (sugar wraps to a second line, the rot line appears), so taps land somewhere else. I opened the log by accident at least four times. I bought Hemicellulase instead of Dormancy Clause and hit EAT LATER instead of buying. *Unfairness.* When the game misreads my tap, I stop trusting any decision I make.
3. **Welcome-back punishment and hidden costs.** Three returns in a row opened with "the floor was bare for 9x% of it. nothing grew while it was." Early on, the floor empties in about a minute at 60 g/s, so the core verb was "buy floor, buy floor". MAX tips silently spent minerals. Ghost Pipe was a hold-to-take with no stated cost beyond labile, and turned out to be a forever 4% tax. *Waiting with nothing to decide, then decisions I couldn't read.*

## 5. The voice and the sound
The voice is the game's best asset. Lines that stuck: "a mushroom is not the organism. it is the part that is leaving." / "you did not deliver. it will not say so to you. it will say so to them." / "it cannot read you, the tree. it signs anyway." / "leaf litter changes hands. hands is a generous word here." / "the beetles were bidding too." It turns spreadsheet events into small stories, and it's why I lasted 10 hours instead of 10 minutes.

But it's also used to deliver error messages, and that backfires: when a button refuses me, I want the rule, not a haiku.

**Sound:** the whole time, the only thing I heard was "no music (sparse sound)". Nothing ever swelled, not even on a patch claim or a 1.2-tonne welcome-back. Silence fits the mood, but milestones felt smaller than they were, and waiting felt like plain waiting.

## 6. How far I got
3 patches (Second Shadow, Windthrow Gap, plus one claim that completed while I was away), 5 trees in the understory, about 2.6 t of biomass, and these unlocks: Standing Order, Dormancy Clause, Osmotic Priming, Mycelial Ledger, The Two-Sided Book (selling), Ghost Pipe Compact, Peroxidase (fallen logs) and bark slough. The biggest change came around **minute 285**. The game went from a hands-on "buy the floor every minute" clicker to an automated economy where my only decisions were contracts and which mineral-gated card to buy next. That was a real shift in kind, and briefly the best part. After it, the game became a minerals wall (654 ∴ for the next patch) behind the contract UI I couldn't operate reliably.

## 7. Coming back
- **30 min / 1 h breaks (early):** a sheet saying I'd gained stuff, then "the floor was bare for 94% / 92%. nothing grew." It felt like a scolding, not a reward. Not worth coming back for, except to restock.
- **2 h break (min 151→271):** "bare 100%, nothing grew", yet +27.7 kg of biomass, which seemed contradictory. Still, 8k sugar to spend made a fun shopping session.
- **3 h break (after Standing Order + Dormancy):** "+1.23 t". The first time coming back felt like a reward. Then I had nothing to spend the labile on.
- **90 min break:** "a claim completed. there is another place under you now." A good line and a good return. Then the next wall.

Once the automation existed, coming back was worth it. Before that, the game punished exactly the play pattern it's built for.

## 8. Would I keep playing tomorrow?
**No.** The writing is good enough that I'd check it once more out of curiosity. But everything left is gated on minerals, minerals come only from contracts, and the contract screen fights me: sliders shift when I scroll, refusals are cryptic, and the auto-buyer drains the sugar the contracts need. I can't see a decision I understand, and that's my quit condition.

## 9. The one change that would make it more fun for me
**Make the contract screen state its rule in plain numbers and stop moving under my thumb.** On SIGN, show one line like "needs 18,000 sugar over the term; you have 8,200" or "max 11.4 with collateral; you're at 13.0". Show coverage based on the same net figure as the sugar sheet. Let me reserve sugar from Standing Order for contracts. Keep the poetry in the log, not in place of the error.
