899 played minutes. The 900-minute cap stopped me. I did not quit. I stopped about fifteen minutes into the most interesting thing the game had shown me, and I would have kept going.

## 3. What was fun
1. **The ledger sheets and ETAs.** Tap BIOMASS or SUGAR and you get "comes from / goes to / net". The "next" line says things like "a tip: 53.3 g short, about 0:57 at this rate". That is a min-maxer's dream: I could compute yield per gram against market price and see that buying litter was profitable. The fun was **optimising** with legible numbers.
2. **Discovering the offline plan.** Standing Order (an auto-buyer that "buys badly and never sleeps") plus Dormancy Clause ("sleep does not break a promise") let me sign big mineral contracts and close the app. Each return was a +1 to +4 tonne haul. The fun was a **clever plan paying off**, though it felt slightly like an exploit.
3. **DECIDE and the second game.** The 12:01 "something is gathering" countdown, the hold-to-take button that "does not undo", the music arriving for the first time, and then a completely new economy with a map. The fun was **surprise, a sense of growth, and mystery**, and the rival "from the north" is a perfect hook.

## 4. What wasn't fun
1. **Contract refusals with hidden reasons.** The coverage bar said "room to spare" and SIGN still refused me 5+ times with rotating flavour text and a "6.25 sug/s at most, 3 seasons" label that didn't match the problem. It was collateral. This was **confusion and unfairness**: hidden costs are exactly what I hate.
2. **Babysitting the bare floor** in the first 2 hours. The litter ran out in about 40 s, there was no auto-buy until 15 kg of labile, and the welcome-back screens said "the floor was bare for 70% / 90% / 100% of it". That was **friction**, with **waiting with nothing to decide** in between. Before the Standing Order, the best play was to keep the app open and tap BUY.
3. **Jittery and contradictory numbers.** The sugar header said +25/s while AFTER FOOD said -2.12 and "0:00 left". Rates swung 5 to 80 g/s from second to second. Refusals said "(0.00 g short)". Buttons sat hidden under EXTEND or the console, so taps silently missed. All **friction**, plus a sense that I can't trust the readout.

## 5. The voice and the sound
The voice is the best thing in the game. Lines that stuck: "you paid for something that was free an hour ago", "paid for. the beetles were bidding too.", "the price went up while you were buying. you did that.", "you did not deliver. it will not say so to you. it will say so to them.", "the good years and the bad years are gone. there is only the average now", and "Something is growing toward you from the north and it is not a tree." It turns dry market mechanics into a story about being a fungus. Poetry as *error messages* is where it hurt: when I need a reason, "the dark holds still. something is missing." is infuriating.
For roughly 880 minutes there was no music. When DECIDE played the lo-fi piece ("keys alone, opening", "the groove", "breakdown, no kick"), it made the moment feel huge, precisely because of the silence before it. It didn't change how I felt about waiting, because there was never music during the waits.

## 6. How far I got
I got from 0 g to 2.28 kilotonnes. I bought every adaptation on the learn tab, got 4 patches, 329 tips and 6 trees (birch, aspen, 2 douglas fir, hemlock, hollow beech), and took DECIDE. The biggest change came at minute 884: DECIDE turned it from a single-floor economy into a forest-scale game, with Signal Σ, a map of 61 stands to colonise, FOREST/MIND tabs, and growth of millions of grams per second. It became something genuinely different, but only in the last 15 minutes of a 15-hour test.

## 7. Coming back
- After 2 h: a "gone 2 h" sheet with +230 kg and honest notes ("the floor was bare for 70% of it"). Clear, and it gave me a pile of labile to spend, which was great.
- After 3 h: sugar capped and rotting, the floor bare 100%, and still enough minerals for Dormancy. Useful, but it told me I had planned badly.
- After 90 min: the floor bare 90%, and a new tree appeared. Mixed.
- After 1 h, 4 h and 2.5 h (with the Standing Order on): huge hauls of tonnes and thousands of minerals, plus "a claim completed. there is another place under you now". Each one was very worth coming back to, with a shopping spree every time.

## 8. Would I keep playing tomorrow?
**Yes.** The post-DECIDE layer and the rival from the north are exactly the hook I want, and the economy is deep enough to optimise. I'd go in wary, though: if the new layer keeps hiding its rules behind poetry, I'll drop it.

## 9. The one change
When an action is refused, show the actual blocking constraint as a number: "collateral 228 kg exceeds the 120 kg this tree will hold", or "you need X sugar banked for this term". Keep the poetry as a second line. Legible failure would have turned my worst 10 minutes into a puzzle I'd enjoy.
