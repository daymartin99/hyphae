# HYPHAE playtest: Jay, commuter (round v2.5-1)

## 1. Stop point
I stopped at **878 played minutes**, close to the 900-minute cap. Over about 15 hours I opened the app 9 times, and each check-in was 1 to 3 game minutes. I quit because I hit the cap. I wasn't bored, and the last two check-ins were the best ones.

## 2. Fun curve

## 3. What was fun
1. **Standing Order and the 1.19 t payoff.** All day the game told me that while I was away "nothing grew". Then one 15 kg upgrade fixed that, and the next time I came back I had more biomass than I'd made in the previous 13 hours put together. That was growth and relief at once, and it felt like the game finally respected how I play. It's the best moment I had.
2. **The Learn shop on a big collect.** I'd come back with 36 kg or 115 kg to spend and tap through three or four adaptations with readable effects ("Halves the moisture penalty", "Winter +55%"). Each check-in had something to buy and visible progress, which is exactly the short loop I want.
3. **Decisions that read like a story.** Perennial Mycelium ("You stop having years... nothing will ever beat the average again") and Autolysis ("You are allowed to be smaller") are mysterious, and they felt like real one-off choices instead of +5% buttons. Taking Perennial was the "one decision, then off" moment I look for.

## 4. What wasn't fun
1. **"The floor was bare for 100% of it. nothing grew while it was."** This came up on almost every return from session 2 to session 7. The food I bought lasted about two minutes, so any real break wasted itself. It felt unfair to someone who only plays in bursts, and it nearly made me drop the game around session 3.
2. **Sugar capped and rotting on return.** I'd open the app to a full bar showing "(rot)", so the income I'd earned while away just leaked. Again, I was punished for being away.
3. **Fiddly contract and floor screens.** I had to scroll through stacked cards, the buttons moved under my thumb, and one mis-tap reordered my floor (EAT LATER). The tree contract has volume, term, collateral, "exclusive" and a coverage bar whose verdict swung from "room to spare" to "you make 0.00 sug/s... this defaults" within seconds. On a bus I just signed blind. That's friction, plus confusion about what happens to me when I'm away.

## 5. The voice and the sound
I never heard any music. Every look said "no music (sparse sound)", so the sound added nothing for me and waiting felt silent. The **voice** did add a lot. The terse lowercase log was the main reason I kept reading. Lines that stuck:
- "it grows while you are not looking at it" (ironic, given the bare floor)
- "the price went up while you were buying. you did that."
- "the good years and the bad years are gone. there is only the average now, and it will not move."
- "it cannot read you, the tree. it signs anyway."
- "tips are cheap. the ground they open is not."

## 6. How far I got
I went from a single thread on a warm forest floor to 61 hyphal tips, two patches, four trees in the understory (birch, aspen, douglas fir, hemlock), fallen logs and stumps as food, a market I can sell back into, and 1.46 tonnes of biomass. The biggest change came at minute ~788 to 878, when Standing Order started buying food for me. The game turned from "babysit the floor or it starves" into "come back to a pile and make choices", which is the game I wanted from the start. By the end the bottleneck was minerals from tree contracts: I had 1.2 t of labile I couldn't spend.

## 7. Coming back
The "WHILE YOU WERE AWAY / gone 2 h" sheet with TAKE IT is a good ritual. Early on it was soured by the orange "floor was bare" line, so coming back felt like being told off. In the middle, the tree contracts paying minerals made returns feel worthwhile. After Standing Order, returning felt great. Every return also meant redoing the same 10+ taps: tips MAX, scroll, litter MAX, scroll, re-sign each tree. That's a chore for a 2-minute window.

## 8. Would I keep playing tomorrow?
**Maybe, leaning yes.** The last two check-ins finally fit my life, and there's a clear next goal (minerals for the Windthrow Gap patch and the Autolysis mystery). If re-signing contracts every visit stays this fiddly, I'll drift off within a few days.

## 9. The one change
**Unlock Standing Order (or some "keep the floor stocked while I'm away" option) in the first hour, not at minute ~788.** Pair it with a one-tap "renew all contracts at last terms" button. Then every check-in becomes collect, one interesting choice, done, which is exactly my 3-minute bus ride.
