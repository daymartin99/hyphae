# HYPHAE playtest: Lee, 27, delivery driver

## 1. Stop point
I quit at **111 played minutes**. Nobody made me stop. By then I'd been broke for about 45 minutes. It was winter, the floor was bare, sugar was sitting at roughly 0–3 and going down, the birch wouldn't trade ("the root turns away. it is not the season."), and the only goal on screen was a patch upgrade I was 33 minerals short of. I had nothing to decide, and putting the phone down for 25 minutes and then 20 more barely changed anything. I signed a tiny contract as my last move and saw it was going to default straight away. That's when I quit.

## 3. What was fun
1. **Eating the whole floor at minute 5.** I'd been mashing EXTEND like any clicker, and then the button went dead with "the floor is bare". I'd eaten the forest, and from then on I had to buy food on a market. That surprise turned a tapper into an economy, and it's the moment I decided to stay.
2. **The market pushing back.** "the price went up while you were buying. you did that." and "paid for. the beetles were bidding too." The price chart moving because I bought was a little optimisation game I didn't expect. It was mastery, and it was funny.
3. **The bargain with the birch, the first time.** Figuring out that ∴ meant minerals, signing a deal, then seeing tips start costing minerals so I *needed* the tree. That was discovery that clicked into something. When I defaulted and the log said "you did not deliver. it will not say so to you. it will say so to them.", and my reputation dropped from 35 to 14, it felt like consequences, not just numbers.

## 4. What wasn't fun
1. **Being stuck broke in winter (minutes 65–111).** I couldn't eat, couldn't buy, couldn't trade, and the one goal was mineral-gated behind a tree that won't talk in winter. This is exactly the "waiting for numbers to go up with no point" I quit games over, except here the numbers weren't even going up.
2. **Silent failures.** SIGN didn't respond at all, several times. The reason was either in a log I had to pull up by accident ("frost. the birch will not talk to anyone until it thaws.") or never shown. "no room in the ground for more." came up after I tapped SIGN, and I still don't know what it meant. Confusing, and it felt unfair.
3. **UI friction.** The header grows as you unlock things (sugar, minerals, after food, owed, rot), so the list shrinks and moves. The EXTEND button slid under the tab bar, and my holds switched tabs. Buying something reflows the list, so my next tap hit the wrong card or EAT LATER. Twice I MAXed tips and spent the labile and minerals I was saving. Bottom sheets (the stat tooltips, the log) wouldn't close by swiping down. On top of that I had to feed litter every two minutes.

## 5. The voice
Mostly it added to it, which surprised me, because I expected pretentious. The lines are short and usually *tell you something* in a mood. The ones that stuck:
- "the price went up while you were buying. you did that."
- "paid for. the beetles were bidding too."
- "a mushroom is not the organism. it is the part that is leaving."
- "you did not deliver. it will not say so to you. it will say so to them."
- "you have eaten everything you were standing on."

Where it hurt: important rules (frost blocks trading, why SIGN failed) were *only* in the voice, scrolled away in a log. Moody is fine. Moody *instead of* telling me why my button didn't work is not. "Autolysis: You are allowed to be smaller… it costs what it looks like" was the one place where the vagueness felt like it was there just to be vague.

## 6. How far I got
I went from 0 g to 421 kg biomass: around 40 tips, 3 floor types, the birch market, seasons, and about 12 adaptations (Reflex Arc, Rhizomorph Cords, Hydrophobic Sheath, Dormancy Clause, Autolysis, Standing Order, Cellulase, Osmotic Priming, Antifreeze, Mycelial Ledger, Foraging Front, Sclerotia, Two-Sided Book). The biggest change was around **minute 5–15**. It stopped being a tapper and became a supply-chain game: buy food on a moving market, turn it into sugar, sell sugar to a tree for minerals, spend minerals on growth. The second change was Autolysis and Standing Order at around minute 35, when it started running itself. I never reached the second patch, which looked like the next big change ("There is more floor than this").

## 7. Would I keep playing tomorrow?
**No.** The first 15 minutes were better than most idle games I've uninstalled. But I left it in a state where I was broke and there was nothing to do, the game didn't give me a way out, and I didn't trust the bargain screen to tell me why things failed. If I opened it tomorrow I'd expect to find the same bare floor.

## 8. The one change that would make it more fun for me
**Never leave me stuck with nothing to decide, and say *why* when something fails.** When I'm broke with a bare floor in winter, give me an obvious lever (dissolve biomass for sugar right there on the grow tab, or let me sell labile back for sugar), and point at it in the "next" line. When SIGN can't go through, put the reason on the button: "frozen until spring, 5:54" or "you can't cover 6 sug/s".
