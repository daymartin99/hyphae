# HYPHAE: Jay's playtest

## 1. Stop point
405 played minutes (about 15 of those were me actually holding the phone, the rest was the phone in my pocket). **I quit.** It wasn't the cap or the tool limit. Two sessions in a row I opened it and nothing had moved: I was still about 10 minerals short of the "Patch" upgrade, and both times the birch deal had gone bust while I was away. That's my uninstall rule.

## 2. Fun curve
| Session | Played min (end) | Fun 1–5 | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 6 | 3 | Tapping, a little thread growing, a tip to buy every 30 s. Then the floor ran out and a finance screen showed up. |
| 2 | 100 | 4 | Came back to 15 kg and a pile of stuff to spend. Hold-to-extend, upgrades, rate went from 5 to 110 g/s. Good bit. |
| 3 | 222 | 3 | 80 kg, bought Standing Order (auto-buy, nice). Then the SIGN button did nothing for three taps and didn't say why. |
| 4 | 313 | 2 | Found out from the log that I'd "defaulted" while I was away. Rep went down. 390 kg labile and nothing to spend it on because it all needs minerals too. |
| 5 | 405 | 1 | Defaulted again, got 0 minerals, still 10 short. Floor bare. Same wall as last time. Done. |

Average: 2.6

## 3. What was fun
1. **Coming back to a big number (session 2).** I left it at 2 kg and came back to 15 kg with a shop's worth of things I could suddenly afford. Classic idle hit: *anticipation and growth*. That's the reason you open these games on the bus.
2. **The first 3 minutes of tapping.** One button, a number going up, a tip to buy every half minute, and the little thread on screen branching out. It was clear and quick. *A sense of growth* you could actually see.
3. **Reflex Arc / Standing Order.** "Hold, and they keep reaching on their own." "Something starts buying on its own." Upgrades that take a chore away feel great in a game I play one-handed. *Mastery and optimising*, of the lazy kind.

## 4. What wasn't fun
1. **The birch contract screen.** Volume, term, collateral, exclusive, coverage bar, "you get ∴/s", rep dots. It's a loan agreement. I skim, and there was nothing to skim. SIGN silently did nothing 2–3 times in a row with no message (I think the max volume was shrinking under the slider, or the coverage was red, but it never told me). *Confusion and friction.*
2. **Defaulting while I was away and only finding out from the log.** I left a deal running and came back to rep cut from 23 to 15 and fewer minerals than promised. The only explanation was buried in the log: "you did not deliver." The second time the after-food sugar went to −3/s seconds after signing ("0:07 left") and nothing warned me before I signed. Standing Order eats all the sugar, so the two upgrades fight each other and the game doesn't say so. *Unfairness and feeling lost.*
3. **Hitting the mineral wall.** I had 390, then 650, then 770 kg of labile and nothing to spend it on, because tips and every upgrade worth having also need minerals, and minerals only come from the tree deals that kept failing. The floor also empties in about a minute and you have to keep re-buying it. *Waiting with nothing to decide, and repetition.* On top of that the layout kept jumping: the tab flipped to "learn" on its own, cards moved as I tapped, and I bought "eat later" and opened the log by accident.

## 5. The voice
Yes, it added a lot. It's the bit that made it feel like more than a number going up. It's short, dry and a bit creepy. Lines that stuck:
- "you paid for something that was free an hour ago" (made me laugh)
- "each press is a millimetre. the forest is measured in them."
- "a contract with something a hundred years older than you."
- "the price went up while you were buying. you did that."
- "you did not deliver. it will not say so to you. it will say so to them." (great line, terrible place to learn I'd been punished)
- "a forest floor is not a floor. it is the top of something." (hooked me, I wanted to see what's under it and never got there)

Funny thing is the voice tells you more than the UI does. "minerals come from the trees, under bargain" in the next-bar was the only reason I found the birch at all.

## 6. How far I got
Biggest change: around minute 5 it went from a tapper to a shop. The floor ran out, I had to buy leaf litter with sugar, and then the birch and minerals turned up. Around minute 220 Standing Order made the buying automatic. It never became something else after that. The "Patch: The Second Shadow" (+1 patch, "there is more floor than this") looked like the next big change, and I spent my last three sessions trying to get 10 more minerals for it and never did. I peaked at 841 kg biomass, about 140 g/s.

## 7. Would I keep playing tomorrow?
**No.** The first half hour of real play was decent and the writing is great. But the second half became "fill in a loan form, come back, find out it failed, try again", and I was stuck at the same 10-mineral gap with a huge pile of labile I couldn't use. Two dead sessions in a row, so it's gone.

## 8. The one change
Make the birch deal impossible to mess up by accident. One "offer a deal" button with a safe default, a plain "this will fail: you only make +1.4 sugar/s" warning *before* SIGN, and a pop-up when I come back if a deal broke ("the birch is angry: you missed payments"). Even better, a small trickle of minerals from something I already own, so I'm never fully stuck behind the tree.
