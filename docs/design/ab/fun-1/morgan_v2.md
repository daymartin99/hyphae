# HYPHAE playtest: Morgan (v2)

## 1. Stop point
I stopped at **157 played minutes**, about 230 tool calls in. I quit; I didn't hit the time cap or the tool limit. For the last hour the economy had stalled. The floor was bare nearly all the time, three mineral contracts with the birch had defaulted, my reputation had gone from 24 to 0 and the first new patch was still 81 minerals away. Meanwhile 270 kg of labile biomass sat there with nothing useful to spend it on. Biomass went from 293 kg to 322 kg over a 30-minute wait. I could work out that the first patch was more than an hour off, and that was assuming nothing defaulted. That was the end for me.

## 3. What was fun
1. **The first 20 minutes of rate maths.** Tip costs climbing 60, 64, 71, 111, 175. "Litter eaten" per tip. Working out that sugar per gram eaten (about 0.16) roughly matched the litter price (0.15–0.20 sug/g), so food is break-even on sugar and the profit is the biomass. That was optimising and discovery together, and it's exactly why I play these games.
2. **Seasons as a system.** Winter had a ×0.32 moisture penalty, so the Hydrophobic Sheath (halves it) and Antifreeze (winter +55%) had calculable value. Then I noticed the birch pays far more minerals per sugar in winter: the volume cap went from 2.9 to 8.8 sug/s and the quote from 0.05 to 0.22 ∴/s. That felt like cracking something, and it was the best moment of the session.
3. **A market that reacts to me.** The price histograms, "the price went up while you were buying. you did that." and leaf litter dropping to 0.09 sug/g when "the leaf market is full". It's a market I can play, not a flat cost curve.

## 4. What wasn't fun
1. **Contracts defaulting with no causal readout.** Three defaults. Each time I could only guess why: the Standing Order taking sugar first, or the floor running bare, which kills sugar income. Nothing told me what the contract draws from, in what order, or how close I was to defaulting. The "shortfall 0:15 left" line sat at 0:15 while sugar was positive, then the contract defaulted anyway. One default cost me 22 reputation. Opaque and unfair.
2. **Numbers that disagree with each other.** The contract preview said 0.22 ∴/s and the live contract showed 0.13. The header showed +0.02 ∴/s while the card showed 0.04. The tip cost said "· 0.00 ∴" until buying ten tips took 8 ∴. "After food" never matched the sugar breakdown sheet. "Coverage" was a red or green bar with no scale, and SIGN failed silently when it was red. In a game like this the numbers are the game. When they don't add up, I can't plan.
3. **A late-game wait with nothing to decide.** Labile went 80 → 150 → 270 kg with no sink: tips needed minerals, the patch needed minerals, and minerals needed sugar I didn't have. The floor was bare nearly the whole time, and I couldn't turn off or tune the Standing Order. Interface friction made it worse. Taps hit the log sheet because the layout shifted between reading the button list and tapping. Sheets were hard to close. The tall header pushed the content into a small window.

## 5. The voice
It mostly added to the game. The log reads like a quiet nature poem that also explains the mechanics, and I liked that pairing. Lines that stuck:
- "you paid for something that was free an hour ago"
- "tips are cheap. the ground they open is not."
- "the price went up while you were buying. you did that."
- "winter is not a pause. it is the same process at a quarter speed, in the dark."
- "a hypha grows only at the tip. everything behind the tip is plumbing."

It went wrong when it replaced information. "you promised more than you are." and "they have stopped asking what you are. they know." are lovely lines. But they were the only explanation of a default, and they told me the consequence without the cause. "the root turns away. it is not the season." came right after I pressed ACCEPT on an offer the game had just shown me. That felt like being trolled.

## 6. How far I got
Five in-game years. Biomass reached 322 kg, with 25–32 tips, 3 of the 4 floor foods plus bark slough, 7 adaptations and the Standing Order, still on patch 1/6. The biggest change came around minute 20–27. The game stopped being "tap and buy tips" and became a two-currency economy: sugar from eating, minerals from tree contracts, a market with memory, and seasons that swing output by 10×. It never turned into a third thing. After that the loop shrank to waiting for autumn.

## 7. Would I keep playing tomorrow?
**Maybe, leaning no.** The systems are deep enough that I want to crack the contract and floor maths. But right now I can't see the variables I'd need, and my last three attempts all defaulted. If an update showed me why, I'd come back. As it is, I'd just be waiting for autumn.

## 8. The one change
**A contract cash-flow readout.** Show, live and before signing: sugar income from eating, what the Standing Order takes, what the contract takes, which gets paid first, and the projected shortfall time ("at current floor stock you default in 3:40"). Also let me pause or cap the Standing Order. With those two things, defaulting becomes a risk I choose and can plan around instead of a mystery.
