# HYPHAE playtest: Jay, commuter

## 1. Stop point
901 game minutes. The 900-minute cap stopped me, not boredom. I'd taken twelve check-ins of about 1 to 5 minutes each, with breaks of 1 to 3 hours in between. I nearly quit around minute 410. I was stuck with no sugar and no minerals, and a contract that said "room to spare" defaulted two minutes after I signed it.

## 3. What was fun
1. **The "WHILE YOU WERE AWAY" card.** Opening the app to "+111 kg" or "+301 ∴" and a big TAKE IT button is exactly my kind of check-in. It's anticipation plus collecting. It works as a reward for being away.
2. **The Dormancy Clause and sleeping contracts.** Once "while you sleep, the contracts hold. nothing defaults in the dark anymore" landed, the game finally fit my life. Make one decision (sign the tree deal), close the app, come back to minerals. That was mastery and a plan, and it made my breaks feel productive instead of punished.
3. **New trees and patches appearing.** First the birch, then an aspen, a douglas fir, and "a second patch of floor darkens at your edge". Each was a small surprise and gave a real sense of the network growing out into a forest. The branching drawing getting bushier helped too.

## 4. What wasn't fun
1. **"the floor ran short. you grew at 1% of the pace you left at."** I saw this on nearly every return. The game says it grows while I'm not looking, then tells me off for not looking. For someone who's away for an hour or more, it feels like being punished for having a life. The haul numbers still went up, but the yellow line felt like a guilt trip.
2. **The default at minute ~410.** The coverage bar said "room to spare" and I defaulted about 2 minutes later, because the Standing Order was eating my sugar. I lost 515 kg of collateral and half my reputation, and the game just said "you did not deliver." That felt unfair and confusing, and I nearly uninstalled.
3. **Getting stuck in the economy, and the fiddly sliders.** Sugar buys food, food makes sugar, and minerals come from trees that need sugar. At one point I had 0 sugar, 18 minerals and 470 kg of labile I couldn't use. The log filled with "(0.00 g short)" / "the root turns away. it is not the season." spam from failed taps. Setting volume, term and collateral took 4 or 5 careful drags every time, and they kept resetting. That's too much friction for a queue.

## 5. The voice and the sound
I never heard any music. Every look said "no music (sparse sound)", so sound didn't change anything for me. The writing carried the mood, and it's the best thing in the game. Lines that stuck:
- "there are two thousand grams of it. you have used ten."
- "you paid for something that was free an hour ago"
- "a mushroom is not the organism. it is the part that is leaving."
- "it cannot read you, the tree. it signs anyway."
- "you did not deliver. it will not say so to you. it will say so to them." (chilling, and also the worst moment)
- "while you sleep, the contracts hold. nothing defaults in the dark anymore." (the best moment)
The voice made waiting feel a bit eerie and alive. But when the same failure lines repeat with "(0.00 g short)" tacked on, it turns into noise.

## 6. How far I got
I went from 0 g to 1.23 tonnes and from 1 tip to 42. I had 3 trees under contract (birch, aspen, douglas fir) and bought "Patch: The Second Shadow". At minute 901 a claim completed: "+ a second place. the same rules, six metres away." Next up was "patch: the windthrow gap". The biggest change came around minute 670. It turned from a tapper/food-buyer into a contracts game played across breaks, which is the version that suits me. The second place opening at 901 felt like the start of a new act, but I only saw its first screen.

## 7. Coming back
- Return 1 (73 min): great. A big number, a new tree and new tabs.
- Return 2 (164): great. +111 kg, and I went shopping.
- Returns 3–5 (226, 347, 409): bad. The floor was bare, "grew at 15% / 1% / 4%", and I was stuck without currency. I was coming back to scolding.
- Returns 6–9 (578–851): okay to good once Dormancy let me leave a contract running. Each one paid minerals and sometimes brought a new tree.
- Return 10 (901): the best. 1.23 t and a new place.
So it was worth coming back to at the start and the end, with a slump of about 6 hours in the middle.

## 8. Would I keep playing tomorrow?
Maybe. The loop I found late (sign a contract, close the app, collect minerals, buy one upgrade) fits a bus ride really well, and the second place has me curious. But the middle of today was rough: everything was starved, I hit the default and I was lost. If tomorrow opens with another "you grew at 2% of the pace", I'll probably drop it.

## 9. The one change
Stop punishing time away. Let the floor (or the Standing Order) keep me fed while I'm gone, or put the Dormancy Clause "nothing defaults in the dark" safety on from the start. Then every return feels like a haul, not "you grew at 1% of the pace you left at."
