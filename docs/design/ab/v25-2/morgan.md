# HYPHAE playtest report: Morgan (min-maxer)

## 1. Stop point
I stopped at **869 played minutes** (away time included). I was close to the 900-minute cap and at about 475 tool calls, so I stopped to leave room for this write-up. That makes it the cap, in practice. I had not quit out of boredom. I was annoyed, though: a fire had just burned a stand I'd invested in, 66 Σ short of the upgrade I was saving for.

## 3. What was fun
1. **The contract screen (bargain).** You pay X sug/s and get Y ∴/s; over the term you get Z; collateral buys "+30% rate · book +278%"; a coverage bar says "room to spare" or "a bad season and it defaults". This is exactly my kind of thing: every lever has a number and the risk is spelled out. Tuning volume against coverage felt like real optimisation.
2. **The compounding tip/floor loop, especially MAX tips with Foraging Front.** At 667 min one MAX spree took me from 93 tips to 238 and from 0.9 to 2.9 kg/s, with the next-tip cost shown in kg and ∴. That's growth I could see, earned by planning.
3. **DECIDE and the act break.** The 10:39 "something is gathering" countdown gave me anticipation. Then came a hold-to-take button with "it does not undo", the giant "STOP TASTING / START", music for the first time, and a new UI with new currencies and a map. That was a real surprise and made the game feel bigger than I'd assumed.

## 4. What wasn't fun
1. **Sugar cap and rot babysitting early on.** Every ~60 s the sugar hit the cap and started rotting, so I had to scroll down and buy floor. That's busywork, not decisions, until the Standing Order arrives about 90 minutes in.
2. **Hidden interactions that hurt.** The Standing Order silently drained the sugar my contracts depended on: coverage read 0 and "0:00 left", and I nearly defaulted twice. The collateral slider defaulted to all my labile with no warning. And the fire at the end took a stand and about 190 Σ of cap with no prior signal I could see. Costs and outcomes I couldn't see are exactly what annoys me.
3. **UI friction.** Cards reflow and the tap targets drift. I opened the log sheet by accident at least five times, tapped the wrong adaptation card, and some slider drags didn't register. Signing one contract took about 6–8 swipes and taps. In act 2, "the forest is 0% you" shows no rate or ETA, and the signal cap meant I was mostly just waiting.

## 5. The voice and the sound
The writing is the best part after the contract maths. Lines that stuck:
- "The oak has been waiting, the way a bank waits."
- "You can afford a liar."
- "winter is not a pause. it is the same process at a quarter speed, in the dark."
- "the beech pays at once and pays well. somewhere in the terms, a long time is waiting."
- "That was the largest thought you will have on this planet." (Ominous, right before the fire.)
- "Nothing is being decomposed. It is being deleted."

The descriptions sometimes carry real mechanical hints ("Read the term length twice"), which I liked. Sound: for nearly 12 hours there was "no music (sparse sound)". The first music was the DECIDE piece (lo-fi keys, 76 bpm, about 3 minutes). It made that moment feel huge, as a reward and an act break. Then it went silent again, and the waiting in act 2 felt flat by comparison.

## 6. How far I got
I reached **act 2 (the FOREST / MIND phase) at about minute 695**, after Action Potential and DECIDE. The game turned into something different: biomass went from tonnes to kilotonnes and MB/s, there's a new Σ signal currency with a cap, a hex map with 14–17 stands to ADVANCE into and make DENSER, and a "the forest is X% you" meter. I colonised Hemlock Lee, Fell Foot and Beech Stand, started Oak Hollow and Long Oak Break, and reached FOREST 1%. I never managed to buy Apical Growth (900 Σ).

## 7. Coming back
Every return started with a "while you were away" card. In act 1 it gave clear numbers: +94 kg, +382 kg, +2.14 t, +4.11 t, +8.06 t. Twice it told me something important: "the floor ran short, you grew at 34% of the pace" and "a claim completed". Both made me want to fix things or spend the windfall. Each return was a satisfying shopping spree with 4–8 new adaptations. It was worth coming back, especially once I had money to burn on MAX tips. The act 2 returns were thinner (just "decomposed" and minerals) and led straight into a cap wall.

## 8. Would I keep playing tomorrow?
**Maybe.** The act 1 economy and the act break hooked me, and I want to know what the hollow beech and "something growing from the north" are. But act 2 so far is "wait for a capped currency, then press DENSER 100 times", and the fire punished me without showing me a risk I could plan around. If tomorrow's session is more of that, I'd uninstall.

## 9. The one change that would make it more fun for me
Expose the hidden risks and drains as numbers before they bite: show the Standing Order's sug/s draw beside contract coverage, show a fire or stand-loss risk % on each stand, and give "the forest is X% you" a rate and an ETA. Then I could plan around them instead of finding out afterwards.
