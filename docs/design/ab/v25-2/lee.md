# HYPHAE playtest: Lee (skeptic)

## 1. Stop point
I stopped at **883 played minutes**, which is the 900-minute cap near enough. I didn't quit. That said, I'd have been close to quitting even without the cap: Act 2 had just stalled behind a "more than you can hold" wall, and I'd been coming back for an hour to see "the forest is 0% you".

## 3. What was fun
1. **The DECIDE moment (min ~810).** A countdown ("something is gathering. 12:09"), then the button I'd tapped for 13 hours changed into "DECIDE. HOLD TO TAKE. It does not undo." Then STOP, music, and a new game. Surprise plus anticipation. It was the only time the game became something else, and the only time I leaned in. It also proved the long build-up had been heading somewhere.
2. **Unlocking new foods (min ~363).** Peroxidase for logs, Deer in the Gully for carrion ("bone is a mineral you do not have to negotiate for"), Laccase for stumps, the Windthrow Gap. Each one added a row to the floor with its own description and its own sug/g. That's discovery, and the upgrade changed what I could do instead of just adding +18%.
3. **Contracts, once I understood them (min ~280-365).** Signing a 964-mineral aspen deal with collateral felt like a real bet. The default line, "you did not deliver. it will not say so to you. it will say so to them.", made the risk feel social and earned. When I set the terms, it was optimising I actually did myself.

## 4. What wasn't fun
1. **The sugar economy was a black box (min 92-185).** Standing Order quietly ate every gram of sugar. The header's sug/s, "after food" and the coverage meter's "you make X" all disagreed. I defaulted on a contract the game had told me was fine a second earlier. Unfair and confusing. I only found the cause by guessing.
2. **Interface friction.** The log sheet opened about five times when I tapped near the log strip, and it was hard to close. Lists re-sorted under my thumb, so I bought Hemicellulase (30 minerals) and EAT LATER by accident. Signing a contract takes three sliders, a scroll and a coverage check, per tree, every time. With six trees I just stopped signing. That's friction where the decisions should be.
3. **The treadmill middle (min ~480-780).** For about five hours of game time, every return was the same: TAKE IT, MAX tips, buy floor, maybe one adaptation, close. Sugar was always capped and rotting when I got back. Labile piled into tonnes with nothing worth buying. The numbers went from kg to t to kt and stopped meaning anything. Repetition, and waiting with nothing to decide. The log even admits it: "a second place. the same rules, six metres away."

## 5. The voice and the sound
The voice is the best thing in the game, and the main reason I didn't delete it at minute 100. Lines that stuck:
- "you paid for something that was free an hour ago"
- "sugar you do not spend does not keep"
- "you did not deliver. it will not say so to you. it will say so to them."
- "four of them are rooted within a metre of you. none arrived by accident."
- "at the edges, your threads are reaching for each other. they have not met." (a real hook, and it paid off)
- "Something is growing toward you from the north and it is not a tree."
- "Stop tasting. Start knowing."

Sound: there was no music at all for 13.5 hours ("sparse sound" every time I checked). At DECIDE a lo-fi 76 bpm groove kicked in for about 3 minutes, and it really did make the moment feel bigger, like a level transition. Then it went quiet again. The silence fits a fungus, but it did nothing for the waiting, and I'd forgotten sound existed until the payoff.

## 6. How far I got
I got to Act 2 (MIND / FOREST) at about **minute 810**. The game swapped tips and floor for a hex map of 61 forest stands, a new Signal (Σ) currency, ADVANCE/DENSER on named stands, and a "the forest is 0% you" progress goal. That was the biggest change, and the only time the game became something different. Before that, the biggest changes were new food types and new trees showing up in the bargain tab (birch, aspen, Douglas firs, hemlock, hollow beech, dying elm).

## 7. Coming back
The "while you were away" sheet is good: clear totals, plus a line like "the floor ran short. you grew at 8% of the pace you left at", which told me what I'd wasted. Early returns felt worth it (big payouts, new trees). In the middle, each return started with "sugar is capped and rotting", which tells me I'm being punished for having a life. Dormancy Clause ("nothing defaults in the dark anymore") was the single best purchase for how I play. The return after Act 2 was the worst: an hour away, and still 0% forest and capped Signal.

## 8. Would I keep playing tomorrow?
**Maybe, leaning no.** The writing and the DECIDE reveal show the designers can surprise me, and "something growing from the north" is a real hook. But Act 2 opened with a cap wall, and the 5-hour middle was a treadmill with fiddly UI. If tomorrow's first check-in is another "more than you can hold", I'm out.

## 9. The one change
Cut the middle and get to the change sooner. Bring the DECIDE / Act 2 moment down from ~13 hours to 3-4. Make each new tree or food type change a rule rather than a multiplier. And replace the three-slider contract form with one-tap "safe / greedy" presets that show where the sugar comes from.
