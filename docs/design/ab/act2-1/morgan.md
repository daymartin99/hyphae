# HYPHAE playtest: Morgan

## 1. Stop point
I quit at **164 played minutes**, by my own choice, well short of the cap and the tool limit. Progress was gated behind minerals (∴). The only source of minerals is the birch contract, and every contract I signed after buying Standing Order defaulted, even small ones. My last one defaulted within 60 seconds while AFTER FOOD read +10 sug/s. The game never told me why, and nothing I changed made a difference.

## 3. What was fun
1. **Hydrophobic Sheath (min ~21).** I saved 1.4 kg of labile for about 11 minutes to buy it, and it more than tripled my income. This is the kind of payoff I play these games for: I spotted the bottleneck from the log line about moisture, planned for it, and it paid out (**optimising, mastery**).
2. **Seasonal market timing.** In summer, 10 kg of litter cost about 1,400 sugar. In autumn it fell to about 160. "Spend everything in October" is a real decision that rewards paying attention, and the log line "the third winter. you knew it was coming and you bought in october" made me feel seen (**anticipation, optimising**).
3. **The economy layers opening up.** Tips, then sugar, then a floor with three foods and EAT SOONER/LATER, then contracts with collateral, then a tech tree with Standing Order, Ledger and Forward Contracts. In the first hour, each unlock arrived just as I had worked out the last one (**discovery, a sense of growth**).

## 4. What wasn't fun
1. **Mineral contracts that default for no visible reason.** Five of seven contracts defaulted. The "delivered" bar was full on some of them, and AFTER FOOD was positive. My best guess is that Standing Order drains sugar to about 0 and any dip counts as a default, but I can't turn Standing Order off or set a reserve. Every default cut my reputation, which worsened the rates and the maximum term. It became a death spiral I couldn't reason my way out of (**confusion, unfairness, strategy with no effect**).
2. **Dead ends with no explanation.** RENEGOTIATE and EXIT did nothing ("not the season", "the reach falls short"). The coverage bar shrank when I lowered the volume, the opposite of what I expected. "No room in the ground" appeared on a bare floor. Signing was refused in winter without saying contracts are seasonal. I can't plan around rules I can't see (**opaque numbers, friction**).
3. **Stalled with nothing to decide.** After about 90 minutes everything useful cost ∴. 330 kg of labile sat idle, my rate fell to about 7 g/s and I was over-eating the patch (79% utilisation, floor always bare). Long waits had no purchase to size them to (**waiting with nothing to decide**). The layout shifting under taps, which cost me my mineral savings, made it worse (**friction**).

## 5. The voice and the sound
The voice is the best thing in the game. It is terse and dry, and it often hides a mechanic inside a line of poetry:
- "each press is a millimetre. the forest is measured in them."
- "other things eat here. that is what a price is."
- "the third winter. you knew it was coming and you bought in october."
- "the price went up while you were buying. you did that."
- "you did not deliver. it will not say so to you. it will say so to them."

The game used that last kind of line instead of explaining the rules, though. When it says "it will say so to them", I want to know how much reputation I lost and why the default happened. Flavour isn't enough when I'm debugging a default.

**Sound:** every look in all 164 minutes reported "no music (sparse sound)". No music ever played, so it didn't make any moment feel bigger or change how waiting felt. I would have loved a musical sting on the Sheath purchase or on the autumn price crash.

## 6. How far I got
I went from 0 g to 357 kg of biomass, with 36–45 tips, eight adaptations (Sheath, Rhizomorph Cords, Dormancy Clause, Cellulase Titre, Antifreeze, Standing Order, Mycelial Ledger, Hemicellulase) and Autolysis, and reached year 5–6. The biggest change came around minute 20–30, when the game turned from a tapper into a market and contract game. I never got the second patch (stuck at 25/90 ∴), so I never saw what I think is the next big shift.

## 7. Would I keep playing tomorrow?
**No.** The first hour was a 4–4.5 for me. But the game's progression gate depends on a contract system whose failure rules I couldn't work out from the screen after seven tries, and each failure made the next one worse. When my strategy stops having any visible effect, I uninstall.

## 8. The one change that would make it more fun for me
**Make the contract maths transparent and controllable.** Show exactly why a contract defaulted (for example, "sugar hit 0 at 1:12; Standing Order spent 340"). Add a sugar reserve floor to Standing Order, and make the coverage bar a plain number ("covers 3:40 of payments at current income"). With that, the mineral layer becomes a puzzle I'd happily spend hours optimising instead of a wall.
