# HYPHAE – Jay's playtest

## 1. Stop point
420 played minutes. That's the cap, so the cap is what stopped me. Being honest though, I was right at the edge of uninstalling after session 4. Sessions 3 and 4 both felt like "come back, floor's bare, sugar's rotting, top it up, put it down again". The only thing that kept me going was the "patch: the second shadow" carrot. I never actually got it. At the very end I had the minerals (98) but ran out of time.

## 2. Fun curve
| Session (played min) | Fun 1–5 | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 0–5 | 3 | Mash EXTEND, buy a tip, the number jumps. Easy to get. Then labile vs sugar vs "the floor" lost me a bit. |
| 95–96 | 3 | Came back to 14 kg and bought 4 upgrades in a row. Rate went from ~7 to 65 g/s, which felt great. Then the birch deal said "not enough" twice and I didn't know why. |
| 216–218 | 2 | Floor bare again, sugar maxed and rotting. The birch deal got rejected 3 times with riddles, the sliders were fiddly, the log popped open by accident twice, and the deal only lasted 6 minutes. |
| 308–309 | 2 | Basically the same visit as last time. Refill, MAX tips, sign birch. Nothing new. |
| 399–400 | 3 | Finally something new: an aspen tree, bark slough as a new food, and a Douglas fir showing up at the end. |

Average about 2.6.

## 3. What was fun
1. **The first two minutes of mashing EXTEND and buying tips.** Tap, number goes up, buy a tip, number goes up faster. It's instant and needs zero reading. I got growth straight away, same as any idle game.
2. **The shopping spree when I came back at 95 min.** I had 14 kg banked and bought Reflex Arc, Rhizomorph Cords, Hydrophobic Sheath and Cellulase in a row, and the rate tripled. That's the "came back to a pile of stuff to spend" feeling I want from an idle game. Anticipation that paid off.
3. **New things turning up late.** Aspen ("eighty trees with one root"), then bark slough, then a Douglas fir. Discovery: it was the first time in hours that the screen looked different from the last visit.

## 4. What wasn't fun
1. **The tree deals (bargain tab).** Volume, term, collateral, exclusive, coverage, ∴/s. I hit SIGN and got "nothing moves. there is not enough of you for that" and "the market shrugs. you cannot cover it", with no plain-English reason. Every time I had to fiddle with sliders until it worked. Confusion and friction, and it's the only way to get minerals, so I couldn't skip it.
2. **Coming back to a bare floor every single time.** It feels like an idle game, but it punishes me for being idle. The litter runs out in minutes, then everything stalls and the sugar sits at max and rots ("−0.8 sug/s (rot)"). Every return started the same chore. That's repetition, and it feels unfair for a game you're meant to put down.
3. **Fiddly screen.** The layout jumps when stuff appears, so I kept hitting the wrong card. I tapped Hemicellulase when I meant Foraging Front, and opened the log sheet by accident twice. EXTEND sits right on top of the tab bar. On a bumpy bus that's a killer.

## 5. The voice and the sound
No music at all. Every time it said "no music (sparse sound)", even when I bought upgrades or signed a deal, so it never made any moment feel bigger. The text has a vibe, and a few lines stuck:
- "each press is a millimetre. the forest is measured in them."
- "you paid for something that was free an hour ago" (after I bought leaf litter, pretty funny)
- "a contract with something a hundred years older than you."
- "four of them are rooted within a metre of you. none arrived by accident."

But I skim, and most of the time the voice was telling me I'd failed something in riddles, like "(0.78 ∴ short)" or "the root turns away. it is not the season." The atmosphere is nice; as instructions it's useless to me.

## 6. How far I got
149 kg biomass, 38+ tips, year 18, three trees in the understory (birch, aspen, douglas fir), bark slough unlocked, 98 minerals banked. The biggest change was money: tips went from costing biomass to also costing minerals, and minerals only come from tree deals, so the game turned from a tapper into a trading game around the first return. It didn't become something actually different from my point of view. The "second shadow" patch was clearly the next big thing, and I never saw it.

## 7. Would I keep playing tomorrow?
**Maybe, leaning no.** I'd open it once to finally buy the second shadow, because I've got the minerals now and want to see what it is. If that's just another number, it's gone. The return loop (bare floor, rotting sugar, fiddly deals) is a chore, not a reward.

## 8. The one change
Make it respect being away. Let the floor auto-refill from sugar (or have a "keep the floor stocked" toggle) so I come back to progress and stuff to spend, not a stalled mushroom and rotting sugar. And give the SIGN button a plain reason when it says no, like "lower volume to X" or a "best deal" button.
