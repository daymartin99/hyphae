# HYPHAE playtest: Lee (27, delivery driver, cynical mobile gamer)

## 1. Stop point
**351 played minutes. I quit.** I didn't hit the cap or the tool limit. After the big "DECIDE" change I ended up stuck. Signal was "saturated" at 933 Σ. Every upgrade I wanted cost more than I could hold (1.4k, 1.6k, 2.4k, 24k Σ). The only way to raise the ceiling was DENSER, and that burns minerals. My minerals sat at 88 with no income ("— ∴/s"), because the trees and the bargain tab were gone. I had 27 kilotonnes of labile and 10 "D of differentiation unspent", and nothing to spend any of it on. That's the "numbers go up with no point" feeling I quit games over.

## 3. What was fun
1. **The DECIDE moment (~257 min).** The screen went black with huge text, music came in, and it came back as a different game: a hex map, new resources, a rival creeping in from the north. That's the one time the game really *surprised* me, and surprise is what keeps me playing.
2. **Bargaining with trees.** A birch "takes what you offer. it does not ask what you are." Sliders, collateral, reputation, and "the one you failed will speak to you again. it has not forgotten." That's a real mechanic with consequences, not another upgrade button. Finding the hollow beech and dying elm deals (14k minerals for 26 sug/s) felt like I'd *optimised* something.
3. **The build-up to the threads meeting.** "your threads are reaching for each other. they have not met" → "they are close now…" → Anastomosis → the view zooms out. That's anticipation done right: it told me a goal was coming without spelling it out, and then it paid off.

## 4. What wasn't fun
1. **The ending wall (320+ min).** Saturated signal, every upgrade "more than you can hold", minerals frozen with no income, 10 D I couldn't spend, labile piling up for nothing. That's waiting with nothing to decide, which is exactly what makes me quit.
2. **Defaults I didn't understand.** I bought floor with MAX, that spent the sugar my contract needed, and I defaulted. Rep went 25 → 4. Later the Standing Order ("pays a little too much and never stops") drained sugar at −355/s right after I sold stuff for sugar. I couldn't find a way to turn it off. It felt unfair because the warning was poetic, not clear.
3. **Friction.** The floor was bare *every* time I came back, so every return started with the same rebuy chore. Contracts had to be re-signed one by one with fiddly sliders. One sign failed because the "max" changed under me (3.43 → 3.47). Lists reorder when you tap, so I bought the wrong thing (Mycelial Ledger) and opened the log sheet by accident about six times. The DECIDE button sat behind the tab bar, so I had to find it in the learn list instead.

## 5. The voice and the sound
The voice is the best thing in the game, and sometimes the most annoying. Lines that stuck:
- "each press is a millimetre. the forest is measured in them."
- "paid for. the beetles were bidding too."
- "the aspen is not a tree. it is eighty trees with one root."
- "you did not deliver. it will not say so to you. it will say so to them."
- "two threads met and did not stop at each other"
- "Something is growing toward you from the north and it is not a tree."

The facts and the threats were great. The vague stuff ("You are allowed to be smaller. It is faster than patience, and it costs what it looks like." / "Stop tasting. Start knowing.") read as pretentious until I actually tried it. For four hours there was **no music at all**, just "sparse sound". The first music was the DECIDE piece (lo-fi, 76 bpm, keys alone → groove → breakdown → groove again). It made that moment feel huge, like a level-up cutscene. Then it went quiet again for the long wait, which made the waiting feel emptier. Music during the waits would've helped.

## 6. How far I got
Biomass went from 0 g to 34.9 kilotonnes (+11 M g/s). Three patches, ten trees bargained with, Autolysis, Anastomosis and DECIDE. **Yes, it became a different game at ~257 min.** It changed from a mushroom clicker with tree-trading into a strategy map: hex stands to advance into, DENSER to push the signal cap, a MIND tech tree, a rival organism, and "FOREST 1%" consumed. The log even switched to capital letters. It had started a third act when it stalled on me.

## 7. Would I keep playing tomorrow?
**Maybe, leaning no.** The DECIDE switch and the thing coming from the north make me curious what happens to the forest. But I left it stuck behind a cap I couldn't see a way past, with a pile of resources that do nothing. If I open it tomorrow and it's still "saturated" with no minerals coming in, I'm deleting it.

## 8. The one change that would make it more fun for me
**Never let me sit at a hard cap with nothing to spend on.** After DECIDE, give me a clear, always-available way to turn my mountain of labile (or my unspent D) into signal capacity or minerals, and tell me plainly what it is. Same fix for the defaults: one clear line ("this will empty your sugar; the birch contract will default") instead of a riddle. Keep the poetry for flavour, not for rules.
