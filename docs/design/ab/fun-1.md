Seven blind agent players took part, five on V2 (`?ui=d`, after #28) and two on **frozen V1** as the control. Each scored fun 1–5 at the end of every session, said why, and named their most and least fun moments. The cap was 7 hours. The brief, reports and traces are in `fun-1/`.

## What is fun
- **The early transformation.** A clicker turns into a market, then into trading with trees. Everyone rated the first session 3–4.
- **The trees as characters, and promises kept.** The dying elm "knows the rate for it". "A mushroom is not the organism. it is the part that is leaving" pays off four hours later as the Fruiting Body. Robin: "it kept its promises." Robin's peak (5) came at the beech, the ghost pipe and the elm.
- **Cracking a system.** Morgan found that winter triples the birch's mineral rate. Sam worked out that minerals gated Anastomosis.
- **The act change.** Sam's 5 came at DECIDE: "the Universal Paperclips moment when the screen you've been optimising turns out to be the small one." The thread hints did their job: "that line sat in the log for about an hour while I worked out that minerals were the gate."

## What isn't fun
- **The dead stretch after the patches** (every player who got there). With 6/6 patches and the shop empty, the voice runs out and resources pile up. Act II is still about 80 tips away, because with 6 patches the thread gate needs about 200 tips. Real players stop buying at roughly 60–120.
- **The mineral economy as a single point of failure** (Jay, Lee, Morgan). Minerals come only from tree deals, so a default or a frost leaves you with "nothing to decide".
- **Tree deals: cause never shown.** Defaults are reported by consequence, not cause. SIGN failed silently. Preview and live rates disagreed. Coverage is unexplained.
- **The layout jumping under the thumb.** Cards expand and collapse, and log lines push SIGN down. On V1 this bought an irreversible card by accident.

## Fixed during the test (V2)
- SIGN always shows its reason, on the button for 3 s and in brackets in the voice.
- Every irreversible card now needs a hold. Twelve could be bought with a single tap.

## The verdict on David's question
The two invested personas had more fun on V2 than on V1: Sam 3.7 v 3.5, Robin 3.7 v 3.0. V2 was the only build where anyone reached Act II, and that player wanted to play again tomorrow. The game is fun until the patches run out. After that, Act I has nothing more to offer, and the door is too far away.
