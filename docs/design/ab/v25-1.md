# v2.5 round 1 (Nov 2026): time away, the away card and the keeper

Five blind agent players played `hyphae-v72`. That build has the away card, the away keeper, the relayed tree lines and the iPhone fixes. The driver gained an `away` command (close the app, reopen it later), and every tester took real breaks. The cap was 15 game-hours, away time included. The brief, reports and traces are in `v25-1/`.

**Targets (11 §Success):** at least 3 of 5 reach Act III; average fun of 3.5 or more; no quits caused by bugs.
**Result:** nobody reached Act III, and only one player reached Act II. Average fun was **3.3**. **One quit was caused by a bug** (Lee: contract refusals), and that bug is now fixed.

On v54, 3 of 5 reached Act II (act2-1); this round, one did. The likely reason is the bug below: minerals come only from contracts, and contracts were refusing people.

## The bug behind the round
**Collateral and exclusive were dropped on every contract signed from the screen.** `core.flowed()` wraps the player's verbs for the ledger's accounts and passed three arguments, while `signContract` takes five. The card previewed the limit with your stake posted, but the tree judged the offer as if nothing was posted and refused under the limit it showed. No stake was ever taken. Four of five testers hit this:
- Lee quit over it ("refuses in poetry when I'm under the limit").
- Sam and Morgan lost about 10 minutes each to it.
- Robin found it by trial and error.

*Fixed in v75*, with a self-test. A second fault was found while reproducing it: the volume slider ignored what was already signed with that tree. *Also fixed.* Sign repro: 6 of 30 legal offers refused before, 0 after.

## What the away work did
- **The card landed.** Everyone described the collect moment as good. Morgan: "each one was very worth coming back to, with a shopping spree every time." Sam: "a claim completed. there is another place under you now" was lovely.
- **The keeper didn't reach the commuter.** It was gated on 25 purchases, and MAX buys count once. Jay came back to "bare 100%" for 13 hours. *Fixed in v73:* the keeper is now gated on sugar spent.
- **The bare-floor line contradicted itself.** It said "nothing grew" above +27 kg (Lee, Robin, Jay). Lee: "it felt like a scolding, not a reward." *Fixed in v73:* the line is now said by outcome, against the pace the player left at.
- **Sugar rotted at the cap during long breaks** (Jay, Robin, Morgan). *Fixed in v73/v74:* while away, the surplus goes to food.
- **"autumn passed" on every return** (Robin). *Fixed in v74:* it is said only for absences under a year.
- **Once Standing Order and Dormancy Clause were bought, returns were the best part of the game for everyone.** Lee: "once the automation existed, coming back was worth it. Before that, the game punished exactly the play pattern it's built for." The keeper is meant to close that gap. Its first real test is the next round.

## Still open
- **The layout moves under the thumb** (Lee, Sam, Jay, Robin). There were wrong-card SIGN taps, accidental log opens and wrong purchases. This was the main friction after the contract bug. It is v2.5 item 4, and it is next.
- **Coverage disagrees with the sugar sheet.** Coverage said "you make 0.00 sug/s … this defaults" while the sheet said +43/s (Lee, Jay). This is v2.5 item 2.
- **Standing Order drains the sugar contracts need, with no explanation** (Robin, Lee). It needs a visible spend, and a reserve for terms.
- **No music in 15 hours** for the four players who stayed in Act I. The first music anyone heard was at DECIDE (Morgan). Act I's big moments (first contract, a new patch, Perennial Mycelium) felt flat.
- **The header takes half the screen** on a phone (Robin).
- **The body view never shows the new patches or the trees you trade with** (Robin).
- **Act I's late game turns into bookkeeping** (Sam): pause, sign, unpause on every return.

## What is fun
- **The voice, for every player, including Lee:** "it's why I lasted 10 hours instead of 10 minutes."
- **The birch's first offer** turning the clicker into a trade game (Sam, Jay).
- **DECIDE.** Morgan's 5: "opened a second game: music for the first time, a 61-stand map." The door still works for anyone who reaches it.
- **Building a plan that pays off while you're away** (Sam, Morgan, Jay).
