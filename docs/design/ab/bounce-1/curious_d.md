# HYPHAE: Sam's session

## 1. Quit point
**56 played minutes.** The floor had been bare since minute 17, the birch "bargain" never did anything I could see in the books, and the next upgrades were 8–9.5 kg away at under 1 g/s.

## 2. What I thought the game was / what I was trying to do
- **Start:** A Dark Room with a fungus in it. Press EXTEND, grow a thread, read the little lines. I was trying to reach the first "tip" (60 g).
- **Middle (min 3–17):** A tidy idle loop. Buy hyphal tips, they eat leaf litter, biomass climbs. Then the reveals came: a hidden SUGAR resource ("you have been making this the whole time"), seasons, a birch nine metres east. I thought I was building toward a partnership with the tree, which is what real fungi do. I loved that.
- **End (min 17–56):** The leaf litter ran out and EXTEND went dead ("the floor is bare"). A **bargain** tab appeared with the birch, a "rep" number and a VOLUME slider in sug/s. I pushed the slider to max. Sugar dipped, my biomass rate dropped from 2.76 to 0.64 g/s, a bar filled and then emptied again, and rep stayed at 15 the whole time. The sugar breakdown never once listed the birch, so I couldn't tell if I was buying, selling or donating. After that I was only waiting for adaptations, and those jumped to 8–9.5 kg.

## 3. Timeline
- **0 min:** Calm beige screen, "the forest floor is warm / it is waiting for you." One button. Good start.
- **1 min:** Tapping gives grams, and three floor types appear (leaf litter 90 kg, needle mat, deadfall). The rate dropped to "—" when I stopped tapping.
- **2 min:** ❓ "a tip is within reach", but where? I tapped the scene and opened the log by accident, then opened "the body" and got a blank full screen. The HYPHAL TIPS card with the +1 button was scrolled off above the floor list. It took me about 6 actions to find the buy button.
- **3 min:** ✦ Bought the first tip: "it grows while you are not looking at it." grow/learn tabs appeared.
- **4 min:** ✦ SUGAR appeared: "you have been making this the whole time." Lovely. As an accountant I noticed litter eaten 3 → 6 g/s but net only 1.50 → 1.77. "other things eat here. that is what a price is." I liked that the books didn't add up on purpose.
- **6 min:** Winter. Intake fell from 15 to 2.7 g/s. "the price of anything left is going up." Seasons were a nice surprise.
- **7 min:** ✦ "wood stored sunlight for sixty million years before anything could eat it." That felt like a promise that I'd learn to eat wood later.
- **7 min:** ❓ The lower list wouldn't scroll any more. The header had grown (biomass/labile/sugar/after food) and the scroll area shrank to a strip.
- **17 min (after 10 min away):** The floor is bare and EXTEND is disabled. The bargain tab appears. Reading the log: "there is a birch nine metres east. its roots are already on yours." That was exciting.
- **17–18 min:** ❓ Birch card: "15 rep", five dots and a VOLUME slider. I nudged it, and nothing changed in sugar. Tapping the card collapsed it. Reflex Arc (1.10 kg) wouldn't buy. It turns out the price is in *labile*, not biomass, and nothing said so.
- **28 min:** Bought Reflex Arc ("hold to extend"), on a floor where EXTEND is disabled, so it was useless right away. Sugar was over its cap and rotting.
- **29–30 min:** ⚠ Volume pushed to max (6.83 sug/s). Biomass rate crashed, rep stayed at 15, and the sugar breakdown showed only "eaten from the floor" and "spoiled past the cap". I genuinely did not know what the birch wanted.
- **45 min:** The log started repeating itself ("empty ground. the growth waits for something to fall, or to be bought." twice). Repetition read as "nothing new will happen."
- **56 min:** ⚠ Bought Hydrophobic Sheath. Cellulase Titre (2.60 kg) appeared and then vanished from the list. The remaining choices were 8.00 kg and 9.50 kg at 0.84 g/s, so hours away. Quit.

## 4. The voice
I read every line, and it's the best thing in the game. It's terse, lower-case and a bit ominous, and it made me *want* the mystery. Lines that stuck:
- "you have been making this the whole time"
- "other things eat here. that is what a price is."
- "wood stored sunlight for sixty million years before anything could eat it."
- "a mushroom is not the organism. it is the part that is leaving." (I took that as a hint about a prestige or fruiting step, but nothing let me act on it.)
- "there is a birch nine metres east. its roots are already on yours."

In the last half hour the voice started to annoy me a little. It kept *telling* me "the market is still there" and "or to be bought" when I was standing in the market, pulling the one lever, and getting nothing back. Then it repeated lines, which killed the sense that the game was going somewhere.

## 5. The single change that would have kept me longer
Make the birch trade readable: a line in the sugar/biomass breakdown that says exactly what I give the birch and what I get back per second, plus some visible change in rep when I trade. If the bargain had paid me something I could see, I'd have had a new engine to tune once the floor ran out, and I'd still be playing.

## 6. Did I ever know how to reach something bigger?
**No.** I got hints that there was something bigger: eating wood (the sixty-million-years line), the mushroom "leaving" (prestige?), Sclerotia ("each one full of a winter"). But I never knew the route. At about minute 17 the obvious engine (eat the floor) ran out, and the replacement (the birch) never showed me how it worked.
