# HYPHAE: Lee's bounce report

## 1. Quit point
**About 9 played minutes.** I ate the whole floor in five minutes, then got stuck waiting. The one new thing, trading with the birch, didn't do anything I could see, and its panel wouldn't scroll far enough to show me the rest of it.

## 2. What I thought the game was / what I was trying to do
- **Start:** a tapping idle game. You're a fungus. Hit EXTEND, the grams go up, buy "tips" to make them go up faster. Easy, I get it, and I knew the goal (60 g for a tip) within ten seconds. Good.
- **Middle:** it got more interesting than I expected. Sugar showed up, the floor was a resource that ran down, and it said "bare soil loses water fast. you ate the cover." I thought the game was going to be about not eating everything too fast (there were EAT SOONER / EAT LATER buttons). I ignored that and ate everything anyway, which is what every other idle game teaches you to do.
- **End:** the floor was empty, EXTEND was greyed out with "the floor is bare", my rate dropped from +14 g/s to +1 g/s, and the game told me "the growth waits for something to fall, or to be bought." So I was supposed to buy food from the birch, but I couldn't work out how. I moved the slider to 2.9 sug/s and my sugar never moved. The buttons list showed an "exclusive" control in there, but I could never scroll to it.

## 3. Timeline
- **0:00** Nearly empty screen, one EXTEND button, "a tip: 60.0 g short." Fine, I know what to do. ✦ Showing the goal straight away was the right call.
- **0:30** Tapped "open the body" and got a full screen of beige with nothing on it. ❓ What was that for?
- **1:00** Tapped 100 times and bought my first tip. The little branching thing grew a bit, which was nice.
- **1:30** LABILE showed up next to BIOMASS. ❓ Which one am I spending? I worked it out from the numbers, not from the game telling me.
- **2:00** A tip says "litter eaten +6.00 g/s" but my real rate at the top is +1.76. ❓ Why are those different numbers?
- **3:00** Sugar showed up (107 / 520). I tapped it and got a sheet saying "only eaten from the floor moves it." OK, but what's sugar *for*?
- **4:00** Reflex Arc in "learn" costs 1.10 kg. I had 1.25 kg of biomass, tapped and held it, and nothing happened. ❓ It turns out it costs labile, not biomass, and nothing tells you that.
- **4:30** "bare soil loses water fast. you ate the cover." ✦ That was a real "oh, wait" moment. My clicking has consequences? That surprised me.
- **5:00** A "bargain" tab and a birch: "there is a birch nine metres east. its roots are already on yours." ✦ Trading with a tree is a cool idea and I wanted to know more.
- **5:30** EXTEND went dead: "the floor is bare." Rate +0.89 g/s. ⚠ This is where the clock started on me.
- **6:00** I moved the birch slider to 2.9 sug/s and my sugar didn't change. I tried scrolling to find a confirm button or a price, and the panel moved about 8 pixels. ❓⚠
- **6:30** I spent 400 g on two tips that added +0.13 g/s. Then: "about 3:35 at this rate."
- **7:00–9:00** I put the phone down for two minutes. I came back to the same screen and the same stuck birch. "empty ground. the growth waits for something to fall, or to be bought." Bought how? Quit.

## 4. The voice
I read them, mostly, because the log is right in the middle of the screen and it's short. Some lines were actually good and did more than the UI did:
- "bare soil loses water fast. you ate the cover." This is the only line that changed how I thought about the game.
- "there is a birch nine metres east. its roots are already on yours." Good hook.
- "nothing left underneath you. other things have food, and a price for it." Clear, and it told me where to look.

Some were mood text that I skimmed and that annoyed me a bit ("wood stored sunlight for sixty million years before anything could eat it", "a pale end, softer than anything around it."). When I'm stuck, a poem isn't help. The voice *pointed* at the birch three times and never once said how the deal works. That's the "vague for the sake of it" thing I hate.

## 5. The single change that would have kept me longer
Make the birch bargain obviously work the first time. Show the whole deal card on screen (what I pay, what I get, a clear accept button), and have my sugar or food number visibly jump when it's active. Right now the most interesting idea in the game is hidden behind a panel that won't scroll, and I hit it at the exact moment I ran out of things to tap.

## 6. Did I ever know how to reach something bigger?
**No.** Around minute 5 I got a hint that there was a bigger game: the birch, "reputation" dots, the 1.10 kg adaptations, the idea that eating the floor too fast hurts you. I never knew how to get from where I was to any of it. The only "bigger" thing I could actually aim for was Reflex Arc, which meant waiting about 15 minutes on a dead rate.
