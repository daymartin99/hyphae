# Jay: HYPHAE bounce report

## 1. Quit point
**185 played minutes, on opening my third session.** (I meant to stop at the 180 cap, but my last two-hour break ran over it. I made the quit call as soon as I picked the phone back up.)
Why: the big button has been dead since minute 4. The game kept telling me to go to "bargain" for minerals, and I couldn't work that screen, so there was nothing left for me to do.

## 2. What I thought the game was / what I was trying to do
- **Start:** tap the big button, number goes up, buy the thing. Basic clicker with a mushroom on it. Fine by me.
- **Middle (2nd session, ~94 min):** I came back to a pile of stuff, which felt good. I bought three upgrades on "learn" and then found a new tab called "bargain" with a birch tree on it. I figured I was meant to trade with the tree somehow.
- **End:** I had no idea. EXTEND just says "the floor is bare". Tips now want something called minerals, and "minerals come from the trees, under bargain". But the bargain card's controls sat underneath the EXTEND button and I couldn't scroll to them. On top of that, the header with biomass, labile, sugar, minerals and "after food" took up half my screen.

## 3. Timeline
- **0 min:** One big EXTEND button and a line saying "it is waiting for you". I tap it and numbers go up. Easy. ✦
- **1 min:** "a tip: 60 g short, about 0:38 at this rate". I like having a target with a countdown. ✦
- **1 min:** Bought the first tip. The game jumped me to a "learn" tab with a 1.10 kg upgrade on it, which I couldn't afford. I went back.
- **2 min:** LABILE and SUGAR showed up next to BIOMASS. ❓ Three numbers, and which one am I spending? I worked out that tips cost labile. I still don't know what sugar is for.
- **3 min:** Tap, buy tip, repeat. Income climbing, little roots getting drawn. This is the 3-minute bit, and I was sort of in.
- **4 min:** I had 1.18 kg and Reflex Arc costs 1.10 kg, but the card stayed grey. When I tapped it, all I got was "the soil does not give. not yet." ❓
- **4 min:** "the floor is bare". EXTEND greyed out and income fell from about 16 to 4 g/s. ⚠ I hadn't done anything wrong, I'd just played. The only thing I could do was the button, and it died.
- **4 min:** Opened "open the body". It's just a big picture of the roots. Closed it.
- **5 min:** Bought one more tip and put the phone down.
- **94 min:** Back. 10 kg, a new "bargain" tab, and rot eating my sugar. Upgrades were green, so I bought three. ✦ Proper idle-game payoff.
- **95 min:** Bargain shows a birch, "15 rep", and VOLUME at 0.00 sug/s. The slider was hidden behind the EXTEND button, and swiping did nothing. ⚠❓ I gave up on it.
- **95 min:** Hit MAX on tips and got 22. Phone down.
- **185 min:** "next: patch: the second shadow: 90.0 ∴ short. minerals come from the trees, under bargain." That's the one screen I couldn't use. EXTEND is still dead. Uninstall.

## 4. The voice
I skimmed it, mostly the line with the ">". Short lines like "it grows while you are not looking at it" were nice and creepy, and gave it a vibe my mate's "weird mushroom game" description promised. Stuff like "something under you is already dead" made me want to see what it was going on about.

It annoyed me when it was being poetic at the exact moment I needed an instruction. "the soil does not give. not yet." told me nothing about why the upgrade I could afford wouldn't buy. "nothing left underneath you. other things have food, and a price for it." meant "go to the trade tab", but I only worked that out two hours later.

The most useful line in the whole game was the plain one at the bottom: "next: a tip: 32.8 g short, about 0:21 at this rate." More like that, please.

## 5. The single change that would have kept me longer
When the floor runs out and EXTEND dies, send me straight to the birch with one plain line like "trade sugar to the tree for minerals". Make sure that card's slider is on screen and not hidden under the EXTEND button. I hit a wall at minute 4, and the way past it was a screen I couldn't use.

## 6. Did I ever know how to reach something bigger?
**No.** The closest I got was at minute 185, when the "next" line said minerals come from the trees under bargain. By then I'd already failed to use that screen once, and it was my second session in a row without knowing what to do, so I was out.
