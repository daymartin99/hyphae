# A/B round 1: current interface vs the redesign (`?ui=d`)

November 2026, at main `59bea6d` (after Phase 6). Run blind by four AI judges. Each judge was a fresh instance that saw only its own folder of screenshots: no source, no history, no key. Kept here so the result outlives the session that ran it.

## Method
- **Same saves, same moments, both interfaces, dark theme, 390 × 844.** The screens are Act I early, Act I an hour in, Act II mid, Act III canopy and the late void. Each transition was captured as eight frames.
- **Labels X/Y were shuffled per moment.** The key is in `round-1-key.json`. Judge 2 received every pair with X and Y swapped, to cancel order bias.
- **Judges 1–2 (comparison)** scored X and Y from 1 to 5 on next, tappable, alive, voice, human (5 = least template-looking) and keep. They also scored each transition on impact, clarity and pull, and picked one version per moment.
- **Judges 3–4 (tasks)** saw one interface per task, counterbalanced, and answered as first-time players with a tap target, a confidence score and the on-screen evidence.

## Result
**Preference: the redesign 14 of 14.** Both comparison judges picked it on all seven moments, under opposite label orders.

**Mean scores** (two judges × five screens):

| | current | redesign |
|---|---|---|
| next move clear | 2.3 | **4.2** |
| tappable vs reading | 2.8 | **3.8** |
| feels alive | 3.0 | **3.4** |
| **voice carries you** | **4.0** | 2.6 |
| made by a person | 3.2 | **3.6** |
| would keep playing | 2.9 | **3.7** |

**Transitions.** The redesign won both, from both judges. Impact was 5 against 2–3, and clarity 4–5 against 2–3.

**Tasks** (confidence of the one judge who saw each version):

| | current | redesign |
|---|---|---|
| a. why are minerals zero | 2 | 4 |
| b. next thing to do, and what stops it | 4 / 2 | 4 / 2 |
| c. what is going to waste | 2 ("can't tell") | **5** |
| d. anything owed or unspent | 2 | 3 |

On task b, both judges named the real blocker (minerals) but guessed at the route to fix it.

## What it says to fix (in priority order)
1. **The voice is squeezed. It is the redesign's one loss, and it is the heart of the game (10 D9).** Both judges found the redesign's narrative cut to one or two clipped lines, while the current interface gave it room. NEXT and the voice are fighting for the same strip.
2. **The DECIDE words are off-voice.** The heavy grotesque type "looks like a Nike ad" against the game's monospace voice.
3. **The route to minerals is invisible in both interfaces.** Players see "no tree is paying" and "1.00 ∴ short" but not that trees, under *bargain*, are the way.
4. **The ending lights read as pickups.** A task judge took the ringed lights for collectibles. They should either open the endings when touched, or stop looking like something to collect.
5. **Smaller issues:**
   - The ledger's split number ("193" next to a tiny grey "17610" next to "kg") reads as a glitch in both interfaces.
   - Act I's first minutes show an empty top third, because the organism is too small to see.
   - The void legend is clipped under the gradient in the redesign.
   - "D" is never explained.

**Caveat.** These are AI judges, so they are good at legibility and visual tells, but they are not players. The real test is people playing both; the interface setting switches between them.
