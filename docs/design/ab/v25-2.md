## What round 2 found, and what was done (v77 to v80)
- **Terms defaulted unfairly once collateral was real** (Jay, 515 kg).
  - A term defaulted after 18 s of non-payment, summed over the whole term.
  - Shortfall built up while away executed the default on the first tick back.
  - *Fixed (v77):* 72 s, a rolling count that full delivery works down, and a cap on shortfall after an absence.
- **A death spiral on a bare floor** (Jay: 0 sugar for three hours). The food reserve dropped to zero on a bare floor, so the terms took every gram. *Fixed (v77):* the reserve holds whenever something will buy.
- **The Standing Order spent the terms' sugar** (four of five testers). The card said "room to spare", the term defaulted, and players paused, signed and unpaused on every return.
  - *Fixed (v78):* the order steps back to the food reserve while any term is behind.
  - *Fixed (v78):* the card and AFTER FOOD now read the floor's need.
  - Economy check with the order: defaults 2 → 0, completed 50 → 50, tips 282 → 268.
- **The log opened from stray taps** (Lee, five times). *Fixed (v79):* a handle in the console's corner opens it.
- **A bought card vanished and the list slid up under a second tap** (Lee). *Fixed (v79):* the card holds its place for 1.5 s, until the thumb is still.
- **The collateral slider remembered 100%** and staked all the labile on the next signing (Morgan). *Fixed (v80):* it resets after every term.
